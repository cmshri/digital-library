ABOUT OUR DIGITAL LIBRARY

Bringing learning resources together in one accessible digital space.

📚

Who We Are

The Digital Library of D.B.R.A. CM SHRI School, Nand Nagri, is an initiative designed to promote technology-enabled learning and provide students and teachers with easy access to educational resources. It brings together academic materials, digital books, learning platforms and useful educational websites in one organised space.

🎯

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make learning more accessible, interactive and engaging. The Digital Library encourages independent learning, research, creativity and digital literacy by connecting learners with reliable educational resources beyond the boundaries of the classroom.

🌐

What We Offer

Students can explore e-books, NCERT resources, digital textbooks, educational videos, question banks, subject-wise study materials, competitive examination resources and government-supported learning platforms.

🚀

Learning Without Limits

Our Digital Library supports flexible learning by helping students discover educational content according to their interests and academic needs. It promotes the effective use of technology for knowledge, exploration and continuous learning.

OUR DIRECTION

OUR VISION & MISSION

Our Vision

To create a digitally empowered learning environment where every student has the opportunity to explore knowledge, develop essential skills and become an independent lifelong learner.

Our Mission

To integrate digital resources into education, encourage responsible technology use, support academic excellence and bridge the gap between traditional learning and modern educational opportunities.
