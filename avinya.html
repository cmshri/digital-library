About "अविnya" — The Mirror of Our School

अविnya is more than a school magazine; it is the living heartbeat of D.B.R.A. CM Shri School, Nand Nagri. Born from the imagination, discipline, and creative courage of our students and educators, every page captures the spirit of curiosity that defines our classrooms.

From reflections on environmental stewardship and Yamuna conservation to explorations of Indian space science, literature, and cultural heritage, अविnya bridges timeless human values with twenty-first-century digital expression.

Why a School Magazine Matters in the Digital Age

When students see their own verses, artwork, and research published in an interactive 3D flipbook, they transition from consumers of knowledge to creators of culture. Our digital edition ensures that every parent, alumnus, teacher, and learner can flip through these treasured memories anytime, anywhere, on any screen.
